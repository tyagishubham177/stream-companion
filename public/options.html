    What leaves this browser
    Public Netflix title IDs are sent in small batches to Wikidata to find their IMDb identities automatically. Wikidata never receives your OMDb key or Netflix account details.
    For films without a direct ID match, the public Netflix title page supplies the release year and movie type. These requests omit account cookies and never include your OMDb key. Public metadata is cached for 30 days.
    Title names, release years, media types or IMDb IDs are sent to OMDb for lookup. Your key is stored in this browser profile and sent only to OMDb to authenticate requests. OMDb also receives normal connection metadata such as your IP address.
    Preferences and saved matches stay local. Browsing accounts, passwords, cookies and playback history are not used.
    Ratings are attributed to IMDb via OMDb. Neither service endorses this extension. Automatic ID matching uses Wikidata. See OMDb’s licence notice.
