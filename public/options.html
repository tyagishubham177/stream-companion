    What leaves this browser
    Public Netflix and JioHotstar title IDs are sent in small batches to Wikidata to find their IMDb identities automatically. Wikidata receives the title IDs.
    Public Netflix title pages can supply the title type, movie release year and credits. These requests omit account cookies and your OMDb key. On JioHotstar, the extension reads title information available on the page.
    Title names, release years, media types or IMDb IDs are sent to OMDb for lookup. Your key is stored in this browser profile and sent only to OMDb to authenticate requests. OMDb also receives normal connection metadata such as your IP address.
    Preferences, saved matches and title marks stay in this Chrome profile.
    Ratings are attributed to IMDb via OMDb. Neither service endorses this extension. Automatic ID matching uses Wikidata. See OMDb’s licence notice.
